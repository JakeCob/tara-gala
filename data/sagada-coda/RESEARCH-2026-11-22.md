## What I added

31 rows in `routes_trips.csv`: 18 from Cubao and 13 from Sagada. Each slot is one row per class Pamasahe lists for it. Two slots take two rows each, because they skip the owner's dates (below).

Every row has `days` = `daily`, `travel_minutes` = 720, `source_url` = Pamasahe, `verified_on` = 2026-11-22, and a note that quotes the source.

- **Classes:** "Bus 35" is `super_deluxe`, ₱1,499, restroom yes. "Bus 45" is `deluxe`, ₱1,304, restroom no. This is the same mapping as the owner's ticket rows, which are unchanged.
- **The owner's slots:** the 22:30 `super_deluxe` from Cubao and the 14:30 `deluxe` from Sagada are each written as two rows, one ending the day before the owner's date and one starting the day after. On 2026-11-25 and 2026-11-28, the owner's ticket row is the only trip in that slot and class. If both rows ran that day, the loader's `_resolve_trip` would find two hand-entered trips at 22:30 and reject `sagada-2026-11-25.json`.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Can you check the real timetable on 12Go or Coda's Facebook page while logged in?** I couldn't reach either. If it's shorter than Pamasahe's 11 + 8 times, delete the rows that don't exist. A wrong bus is worse than a missing one.
2. **What did your tickets actually cost?** If they weren't ₱1,499 (out) and ₱1,304 (back), the fares here need updating, maybe for a fuel surcharge.
3. **Is the 2:30 PM from Sagada really both classes?** You booked the 45-seater. I added a 35-seater row too, because Pamasahe says "Bus 35 and 45".
4. **Keep the date split on the 22:30 and 14:30 rows, or drop it?** The split keeps your booked rows as the only trips in those slots on your dates. After Nov 28 you could delete your ticket rows and the split, and leave one plain daily row each.
5. **Should the regular rows get a `valid_to`, such as a recheck date?** They're open-ended now, so they won't drop out by themselves if Coda changes the timetable.

## Is a Sagada ready-made trip possible now?

**Yes, as far as the data goes.** On a local throwaway database (`tg_ovn_data`: schema, migrations, both seeds, `data/*.csv`, then `data/sagada-coda/`), I dry-ran a scratch entry with `load_premade.py --dry-run --file <scratch>`. It went out Wed 2026-12-02 at 21:00 `super_deluxe`, came back Sat 2026-12-05 at 14:00 `deluxe`, and used town-centre walking legs (Echo Valley, Hanging Coffins, Sumaguing, Yoghurt House). The result was "Dry run OK, nothing saved. Would load 1 pre-made itinerary". The entry isn't committed.

Before writing a real one:
- Pick buses from the higher-confidence rows (20:00, 21:00 or 22:30 out; 14:00, 14:30 or 15:00 back).
- A ready-made trip is fixed to dates, so it goes stale like the Baguio ones. Re-run `load_premade.py --dry-run` after any data change.
- Most Sagada spots have no `visit_minutes` or walking times, and the van legs are per-group hires. A plan built on them is coarser than the Baguio ones.
- Decide whether a public plan should name a lodge. The town-centre hub works without one.
- The owner's question 1 should be answered first, so the plan doesn't depend on a Pamasahe-only bus.
